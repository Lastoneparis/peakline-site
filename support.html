Support

It counted a lift as a run (or missed one)

Detection reads vertical change against time. A slow gondola, a very short drag lift, a long flat traverse or a cat track can sit in the ambiguous band. Open the day and correct it — the session totals recompute from the corrected runs.

My vertical looks wrong by a hundred metres

Altitude comes mainly from the barometric altimeter, which is precise about change but drifts with the weather over a day. A passing front can move the baseline. Peakline is generally accurate about how much you descended and less certain about the absolute altitude at any single moment.

Nothing recorded

Peakline needs Location permission set to Always, because a day continues while the phone is in a pocket and the screen is off. Check Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services, and also check that Low Power Mode did not come on mid-morning.

Battery

Continuous GPS for six hours in the cold is expensive on any phone. Keep the phone in an inside pocket rather than an outer one; lithium cells lose usable capacity long before they lose charge.

Where is my data

On the device. There is no account, no upload and no server. Deleting the app deletes the history with it, so export or screenshot anything you want to keep first.

Anything else

contact@oshi-messenger.com — the date of the day in question and the resort help a lot.
